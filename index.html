about.md

I'm Savindu, a coder from Sri Lanka. I came up through competitive programming and was selected for Sri Lanka's 2025 National IOI Team — that track taught me how to think in algorithms.

I built my early stuff in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, then moved into C++, Python, and Bash, which is what I work in day to day now.

My setup is split: iSH on an iPhone 11 for a terminal on the go, and Kali Linux on my laptop for actual security work.

Right now I'm doing my Advanced Levels (Maths, Physics, ICT), and most of my free time outside that goes into cybersecurity and hacking — algorithms stay in the mix, just at a lower dose than before.
