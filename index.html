about.md

I'm Savindu, a coder from Sri Lanka who started out of pure curiosity and never really stopped. I didn't follow a roadmap — I just kept poking at things until they made sense.

Most of my time goes into Python, C++, and Bash. Python is where I'm comfortable. C++ is where I'm pushing hardest right now, mostly through competitive programming. Bash is what I reach for the moment I catch myself doing something twice.

I run most of my dev work out of Termux — a full environment off a phone. Working inside that kind of constraint forces you to actually understand your tools instead of leaning on defaults.

Lately that curiosity has pointed toward cybersecurity. Not as a label, just because understanding how systems fail is the most honest way to understand how they work.
